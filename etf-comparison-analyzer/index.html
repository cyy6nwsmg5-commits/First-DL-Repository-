Methodology

  1. Each ETF is scored 1–10 in five categories — Technicals (RSI positioning + MACD signal quality), Fundamentals (valuation, cost, yield efficiency), Momentum (trailing returns + moving‑average trend), Price Targets (upside implied by benchmark/analyst outlooks), and Earnings (growth and quality of underlying holdings' earnings).
  2. Scores are derived from the sourced data below, not computed live from a market feed — treat them as a structured snapshot, not a real-time signal.
  3. The default view weights all five categories equally (20% each). Moving a slider re-normalizes all five weights to sum to 100% and recalculates each fund's overall score as a weighted average of its category scores, scaled to 100.
  4. The investor-profile buttons are preset weightings — "Growth" leans on momentum/earnings, "Income/Value" leans on fundamentals, "Technical Trader" leans on technicals/momentum — so the "best buy" answer is explicitly conditional on what you're optimizing for, not a single universal answer.
